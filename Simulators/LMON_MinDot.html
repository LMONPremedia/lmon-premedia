🦉 OTTO TIP

Choosing the right minimum dot depends on your press, paper and printing conditions. Uncoated and newsprint stocks absorb more ink, so dots spread more — a higher minimum dot helps. On coated stocks with tighter dot gain, a lower setting like 1–2% is usually enough. Always verify with a press proof before committing to production.

💡 DID YOU KNOW?

  • Typical minimum dot values range from 1% to 3% depending on the press.
  • Without a minimum dot, 0.5% tones can completely disappear on press.
  • Flexographic printing often needs higher minimum dot settings than offset.
  • ICC profiles and RIP software can apply minimum dot compensation automatically.
  • Setting minimum dot too high can cause highlights to look heavier than intended.
