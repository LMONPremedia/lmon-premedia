💡
TIP: Use angles as far apart as possible. Standard CMYK angles (C:15°, M:75°, Y:0°, K:45°) minimise interference and produce a stable rosette. Avoid placing two dominant inks at similar angles.

🦉 OTTO TIP

The most visually dominant inks — Black and Magenta — should be placed at the most visually separated angles (45° and 75°). Yellow, being the least visible, is typically placed at 0° where any slight interference is hardest to see. Try the Poor Example preset to see what happens when angles are too close together.

💡 DID YOU KNOW?

  • ISO standard screen angles (C:15°, M:75°, Y:0°, K:45°) have been used in offset lithography for decades.
  • The rosette pattern is intentional — it is a sign of correct angle separation.
  • Flexographic printing often uses different angle sets due to its dot shape and press characteristics.
  • Stochastic (FM) screening uses random dot placement and does not require angle separation.
  • When two inks share the same angle, a visible stripe pattern appears instead of a rosette.
